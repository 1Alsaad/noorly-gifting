Date: 23 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm

This follows the [offer and presale decision](Noorly_Offer_and_Presale_Decision_2026-10-23.md) and [authenticated supplier qualification](Noorly_Domain_Checkout_and_Wooden_Kit_Qualification_2026-10-23.md). Research used the owner's connected Chrome. Figures distinguish observed prices from untested packing and freight scenarios. No purchase, supplier contact or launch was made.

## Recommendation in plain terms

Keep the sculptural wooden bowl and one sealed scented sand pack. The best first saving is a compact, properly protected parcel, with plain packaging and batch preparation in Sweden. Do not buy larger quantities merely to obtain a discount.

Prototype the 1 kg configuration first. If its finished parcel qualifies for the lower delivery band, retain 1 kg: the extra 350 g costs only about SEK 17. If 650 g is needed to get below the delivery threshold, and provides adequate fill and appearance, use that version. The quoted 650 g product is Rhubarb Sparkle; the 1 kg quote is Midnight. They are different scents, not interchangeable quotations.

A cost near SEK 545 per 650 g kit is a conditional planning target, compared with SEK 581 for the original heavier 1 kg model. About SEK 538 requires achieving the shorter handling time too. About SEK 509 additionally requires a carrier quote of SEK 80 including all tax and surcharges; that rate has NOT been obtained.

Do not reduce the price by SEK 100 to save SEK 40. Keep SEK 1,149 delivered as the 1 kg price hypothesis and SEK 1,099 for the 650 g alternative, subject to demand and full product qualification. Choose one launch configuration.

Business prices are displayed ex VAT, so an SEK 80 ex-VAT quote would cost SEK 100 if that tax is nonrecoverable.

Request/obtain a real business quote only after measuring the package. Compare pickup-point services using the same dimensions, weight, destination and inclusions, including applicable surcharges and collection/drop-off costs. SEK 80 cash is a target for sensitivity, not an available carrier offer.

Charging the customer separately for delivery changes revenue presentation, not the carrier cost. Preserve total delivered-price comparisons. Consolidated supplier freight is already included in the batch model; do not count that saving twice.

## 4. Handling: batch twenty, then time the work

Use one bowl size, one qualified insert, one scent and one pack size. Prepare labels and instructions together; receive and inspect components together; use a repeatable pack layout; dispatch the completed batch together within the promised window.

Current allowance is six minutes at SEK 200/hour = SEK 20 per kit. A four-minute target would be SEK 13.33, saving SEK 6.67. This has not been achieved or timed. Include allocated receiving, quality checks, label preparation, packing and dispatch time; a separate drop-off trip must not disappear from the calculation. Keep six minutes until a representative batch supports the reduction. Value the owner's time even if no wage is paid out immediately.
