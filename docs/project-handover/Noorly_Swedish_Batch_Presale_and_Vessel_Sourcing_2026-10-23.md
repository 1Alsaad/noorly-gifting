# Noorly candle sand batch fulfilment and vessel sourcing

Updated 23 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm. Research and operating proposal only. No presales, spending, orders, accounts or live store settings were activated.

## Decision

The owner's proposed model is worth qualifying: take a small, clearly dated presale, buy finished retail pouches and candle vessels in a consolidated batch, then pack and dispatch from Sweden. Physical inventory need not precede every customer order, but a representative sample, credible stock/dispatch evidence, a complete delivered-cost calculation and working payment/refund flow should precede a paid launch.

Reassess candle sand under this model before selecting a replacement product. The AliExpress candidate ranking was research, not an approved pivot.

A three- or four-times pouch price can produce useful product margin. It is not net profit, and a bowl changes the goods, weight, packing and damage costs.

## Consolidated freight and domestic delivery

AW's current public Sweden tariff lists EUR 9.60 for 0–2 kg and EUR 17.00 for 10–30 kg. For illustration, 50 pouches have 15 kg net contents. If the actual packed consignment qualifies for one 10–30 kg parcel, inbound freight at that table rate would be EUR 0.34 per pouch. Goods at EUR 6.25 each plus EUR 17 freight total EUR 329.50 before applicable tax and other costs. This is a tariff illustration, not a reserved basket or final quote.

A bowl supplier introduces another inbound consignment unless bought together with the pouches. All inbound costs must be allocated across the actual sellable kits.

Domestic parcel delivery, protective packaging, packing time, damaged glass and returns remain. DHL's public private-customer table lists 73 SEK up to 1 kg and 99 SEK over 1–2 kg, with 1–3 working days domestic transit. PostNord's public private table lists 115 SEK for a 1 kg Service Point parcel. These are retail benchmarks, not a verified business contract or Noorly quote. Confirm merchant eligibility, VAT basis, packed dimensions/weight, surcharges, insurance and pickup-point versus home delivery.

Savings formula per order:

direct supplier delivery cost minus [allocated batch freight + Swedish delivery + incremental packing/handling + extra damage cost].

Do not model the repeated international fee as disappearing without replacing it with domestic fulfilment costs. Use a Swedish business/broker quote before choosing the final customer delivery price.

## Proposed kit and operating plan

Initial concept: one approved scented pouch, its included wicks, one approved vessel and Swedish use/care information. Offer no more than two actual scent choices and one vessel in the first batch. Keep refill-only and complete-kit economics separate.

At AW and clear-bowl published prices, component goods alone are EUR 6.25 + EUR 3.07 = EUR 9.32 ex VAT per one-pouch kit, before two suppliers' freight, packaging, labour and customer delivery. Twenty such kits imply EUR 186.40 in component goods; fifty imply EUR 466.00. These are conditional AW-based illustrations with zero AW stock today, not purchase-ready quotes.

Do not determine kit retail by multiplying only the sand cost. Model the full kit at the intended delivered price and several CAC assumptions. No new final retail price is selected here.

Proposed limited paid-pilot sequence, after readiness is established:

1. Verify exact stock and dispatch terms; approve the sample and the complete sand/wick/vessel combination. Obtain the actual Swedish parcel quote from a weighed pack.
2. Publish a clearly identified presale with a credible customer delivery window, exact contents, total charges, payment timing, cancellation/refund route and order cap. Any all-or-nothing minimum would need to be explicit; it is not assumed.
3. Cap the first small batch around 10–20 customer orders and close the short presale window after roughly 3–5 days, or earlier if funded and fulfilment-ready. These are planning numbers, not a scheduled launch.
4. Place a consolidated order promptly within the disclosed timetable. Do not wait indefinitely for a discount threshold. Check stock again before committing.
5. Receive and inspect in Sweden; protect the vessel, pack, dispatch with tracking, and retain a small approved replacement/refund buffer.
6. Reconcile actual contribution and delivery results before another batch. No new media allocation or reinvestment permission follows automatically.

No new website changes are proposed merely to demonstrate the concept. Keep the restored video storefront and owner access intact while qualification continues.

## Presale requirements and cash timing

Presales can precede bulk inventory purchasing. Shopify documents full, partial or no initial payment through its preorder setup; the supported merchant route requires a preorder app and Shopify Payments or PayPal Express. The current custom storefront needs an actual compatible end-to-end test. Changing a button or allowing overselling is not evidence of a working deferred-payment flow.

A payment authorization is not settled cash. Shopify's Sweden guidance states a three-business-day minimum settlement, longer initially, plus typical bank processing of 24–72 hours. Supplier payment is due on ordering. A pilot must be fundable even if receipts arrive later than the supplier invoice. VAT and refund liabilities must be retained rather than recycled into ads.

Konsumentverket describes delay relative to the agreed delivery time; absent a promised time, the default is 30 days. A preorder label does not remove delivery responsibility. Distance-selling withdrawal rights normally run 14 days from receipt. Use clear pre-purchase information and an operational cancellation/refund process, including the applicable online withdrawal function.

The last saved implementation evidence still lacks a complete checkout/payment/supplier/paid-Purchase test. Those dynamic settings were not freshly reverified here. Today's Friday checkpoint is not an unconditional release deadline. Keep commerce closed pending the specific launch gates and explicit activation instruction.

The total existing media allocation remains 4,000 SEK, with the standing up-to-2,000 initial screen and 2,000 reserve. Inventory, samples, freight and returns working capital are separate; none was newly approved.
