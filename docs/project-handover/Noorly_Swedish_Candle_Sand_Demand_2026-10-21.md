# Swedish scented candle-sand demand and one-month cash model

Research date: 21 October 2026. Scope: Swedish demand for scented ljussand/sandljus, especially a proposed pair of Candledust 350 g refills. This is research and scenario arithmetic, not a launch, price or spending approval.

## Assessment

There is evidence of Swedish customer interest and buying experiences, but no defensible estimate of market size, Noorly conversion rate or customer acquisition cost. Public evidence supports a small validation test once readiness gates pass. It does not establish a fast-growing market, demand at 599 SEK, or a probable 100k November result.

The strongest newly identified obstacle is same-product direct pricing. Reassess the proposed 599 SEK delivered pair before spending. Lowering price also lowers allowable CAC; improving conversion alone does not guarantee profit.

## Search interest

Inspected the live Google Trends accessible chart in connected Chrome: Sweden, past five years, Web Search, all categories, search terms “ljussand”, “sandljus” and “candle sand”.

[Reproducible comparison](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=SE&q=ljussand,sandljus,candle%20sand).

The weekly series is sparse, with many displayed zeros and isolated spikes. Sandljus showed a cluster in November–December 2024, including a normalized peak of 100 in the week starting 24 November 2024. That is a relative index on this comparison, not 100 searches. The view did not demonstrate a sustained recent rise or a repeatable annual seasonal pattern. Related-query panels for the Swedish terms reported insufficient data. The current week is incomplete.

[Google's methodology](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en) explains that Trends uses sampled, normalized data; low-volume searches can display zero. Do not infer zero demand, absolute monthly search volume, forecast sales or statistically significant growth from this chart. English “candle sand” can introduce unrelated query noise.

## Customer evidence

- [Sandig Trustpilot](https://se.trustpilot.com/review/sandig.se): snapshot displayed 18 reviews, 4.1 rating and nine reviews in the last 12 months. Several carry verified-interaction labels. Positive experiences mention scent and creative use; individual negative reviews mention cost/value and unclear sample contents. These are customer-experience signals, not audited sales totals or a representative population sample. A verified label confirms an interaction, not every claim.
- [Sandig scented product](https://www.sandig.se/products/sandljus-med-doft): displayed 55 on-site reviews, 500 g/30 wicks and a buy-one-get-one promotion. A reliable price was not extracted. Do not infer sales volume or compare an unknown delivered price.
- [Veloni](https://veloni.se/products/veloni-sandljus): displayed a from-price of 189 SEK, multiple pack sizes and on-site reviews. The exact selected weight/delivered basket was not verified. Not a like-for-like comparison.
- [AuraCandles](https://auracandles.se/): displayed on-site reviews and Christmas-gifting presentation, with 399 SEK Pearls and 799 SEK gift-set cards. Exact comparable weights and checkout totals were not verified. Site-hosted reviews and retailer marketing do not prove market size or seasonal uplift.
- [Eld och Design](https://eldochdesign.se/butik/indoor-living/ljus/candle-sand-ljussand-stearin-650g/): unscented 650 g with ten wicks advertised at 179 SEK before delivery. This is adjacent budget competition, not an equivalent scented pair.

No competitor safety, environmental, burn-time or delivery claim is adopted as Noorly copy.

## Direct price pressure

[Candledust's public refill category](https://thecandledust.com/product-category/candles/refill-pack-for-glass-candle/) advertised Nordic Lights and Midnight scented 350 g refills at €16.90 each. The page displayed free EU shipping from €25 and a buy-three/pay-two banner.

Two full-price pouches total €33.80, approximately 375.96 SEK at the existing planning FX of 11.123 SEK/EUR. This is NOT a live exchange quote or a verified Swedish delivered checkout total: destination VAT, shipping eligibility and promotion conditions were not tested. Nevertheless, the proposed 599 SEK pair is approximately 59% above that advertised product subtotal. A simple resale pair needs a credible, costed reason for the premium. No vessel, gift box, card or service should be implied unless actually supplied.

The authenticated Hertwill cost model remains about 230.80 SEK goods plus assumed shared delivery, before unconfirmed charges, exceeding the 200–220 SEK target. Pack-weight/SKU discrepancy, exact mixed-pair shipping and product documentation remain unresolved. Await the already-approved supplier inquiries; no further outreach was sent for this research.

## One-month reinvestment sensitivity

Same assumptions as v1.4: 599 SEK collected per pair; assumed 25% VAT; 230.80 SEK goods/shared delivery; 31 SEK payment/refund allowance. Contribution before ads and extras is 217.40 SEK/order.

For illustration only, begin with a complete 4,000 SEK media wave AFTER the initial 2,000 SEK screen gate and justified use of the reserve. Then recycle all settled cash left after VAT, goods/delivery and payment/refund allowances into one further media wave at unchanged CAC. This includes recovering and reusing initial advertising capital, not merely incremental profit. Fractional expected orders are rounded.

- CAC 180: approximately 29.4k SEK collections, 8.83k cumulative media, 1.83k contribution after media.
- CAC 150: approximately 39.1k SEK collections, 9.80k cumulative media, 4.40k contribution after media.
- CAC 120: approximately 56.1k SEK collections, 11.25k cumulative media, 9.13k contribution after media.

All contribution figures precede fixed costs, unconfirmed fulfilment/FX charges, owner pay and relevant taxes. They are not net profit. CAC is hypothetical, not measured or claimed as a benchmark.

At CAC 120, three full waves mathematically exceed 100k collections, but require fast settlement, enough conversion time, stable acquisition efficiency, stock and fulfilment capacity. That is an upside sensitivity, not a base forecast.

[Shopify Sweden payouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/sweden/payouts) states minimum settlement of three business days, a longer initial settlement and additional bank processing. Noorly payment setup is incomplete and its actual timing is unverified. Supplier costs may fall due before payouts: the first approximately 27 orders at CAC 150 imply about 6,155 SEK goods/shipping, requiring a cash-timing plan beyond the media allocation.

## Decision implications

The earlier 30–40k first-month planning target is explicitly conditional and must not be called the most likely outcome. Public demand findings do not establish a probability range. A failed first screen can yield little or no revenue and lose its media allowance plus preparation costs.

Before any authorised test, settle the value/price gap, written landed costs, safety/weight/SKU details, truthful delivery terms and complete checkout/payment/fulfilment/consent/Meta verification. Use verified purchases and contribution after a reasonable conversion-delay review. Clicks or active competitor ads alone are insufficient.

Exactly 4,000 SEK total media remains the standing cap, with an initial 2,000 SEK screen and 2,000 SEK conditional reserve. Recycled spending above that total requires separate authorisation. No ads, paid presales, supplier orders, paid apps or payment tests were activated.
