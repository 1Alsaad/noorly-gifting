# Noorly: private-label candle sand and complete-kit economics
Verified 23 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm. Research in the owner's connected Chrome session; no supplier contacted and no purchase made.

## Decision

Private-label candle sand is feasible at small quantities, but no supplier has yet quoted a cheaper, finished, scented 650 g Noorly pack delivered to Sweden at the proposed twenty-kit pilot quantity.

The most useful newly verified facts are:
- The Candledust explicitly permits its bulk sand to be repacked under the buyer's brand. The signed-in account produced actual Sweden freight/tax baskets for 14 kg and 25 kg. This is a genuine own-brand route, not permission inferred from ordinary resale.
- At twenty kits, bulk sand plus paid filling/labels/pouches is provisionally **more expensive** than the previous sealed branded packs.
- At 25 kg, allowing 37 usable 650 g kits, the model saves about **SEK 16.57 per kit**, or **SEK 331 in a twenty-sale month** on an inventory/expense basis. It requires more cash upfront; unsold sand is stock, not spendable profit.
- PJ Nordic in Denmark, M1 Candles in Belgium and Devillebard in France are the best nearby quote leads. Their complete private-label pilot prices and exact minimums are not public. No invented wholesale prices have been assigned.
- The Swedish manufacturers inspected advertise ordinary poured candles; none has confirmed candle-sand production. Sandig's previously found reseller programme is not established private label.
- A ready-filled, labelled, delivered 650 g Noorly pack at **SEK 100 or less**, including suitable wicks and nonrecoverable tax, is a useful buying target. SEK 80 would be better. These are targets, not offers.

Keep the sculptural bowl and proposed glass insert in the cost model. Do not improve the arithmetic by silently reducing sand, removing the insert or assuming the wooden bowl is safe for direct burning.

### Profit and cash are different

- Twenty-kit 14 kg preparation: approximately **SEK 3,345**, before bowls/inserts/fulfilment/ads/overhead.

## Recommended action

1. Obtain complete factory-filled private-label quotes from PJ Nordic, M1 and Devillebard for one existing white scent, a plain pouch plus Noorly sticker, 650 g and suitable wicks. Avoid a custom fragrance or printed pouch run.
2. Require a delivered finished-pack target of at most SEK 100; compare SEK 80 as a stretch goal. Record tax, setup, packaging, filling, labelling, sample and freight separately.
3. Obtain a binding local filling/fulfilment quote before choosing the bulk alternative. SEK 15 filling and the old warehouse calculator are not commitments.
4. Retain the 14 kg route as a small own-brand fallback if branding is worth its extra cost. Do not buy 25 kg merely for a discount until sufficient demand and packaging/service feasibility are established.
5. Preserve the twenty-order pilot scope. Any paid presale still requires owner authorization, product/insert testing, a confirmed supply date and a workable business/tax setup.
