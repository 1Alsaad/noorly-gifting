# Noorly health assessment and Swedish candle-sand options
Date: 23 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm

## Commercial verdict

**Promising enough to qualify and test a small offer, but not yet a demonstrated healthy business.** The previous positive figures are conditional calculations, not measured demand or supplier commitments. The owner is not yet registered as a business or for VAT. Sales exemption eligibility and the appropriate longer-term tax model are unresolved.

The [monthly model](Noorly_Monthly_Breakeven_2026-10-23.md) uses SEK 1,099 delivered for the 650 g kit, provisional outsourced variable cost SEK 535.43, assumed fee 3% + SEK 3, total monthly overhead SEK 2,000 and total monthly ads SEK 3,000. Advertising is deducted once.

At twenty orders:
- Customer collections SEK 21,980.
- Eligible exempt scenario: about SEK 5,552 surplus before income tax, roughly 25.3% of collections after the budgeted monthly expenses.
- Pessimistic output-VAT/no-input-recovery stress: about SEK 1,156, roughly 5.3% of collections.
- Twenty orders from SEK 3,000 ads requires SEK 150 ad spend per retained customer. That performance is unproven.

At thirty orders, the model gives SEK 10,828 exempt / SEK 4,234 stress. It requires SEK 100 ads per retained order if all thirty are paid acquisitions. Quantities above the twenty-order pilot are sensitivity scenarios, not buying or activation authorization.

The exempt case looks attractive on paper. The low stress result has little room for unquoted assembly fees, breakage or worse acquisition. The stress column is not a finished registered-business calculation because it does not recover eligible purchase VAT. Do not select a tax scenario merely to make the offer look good.

Commercially, do not rely on a temporary small-business exemption to justify a product intended to scale. Obtain a proper invoice/tax calculation and genuine delivered fulfilment quote, then judge the observed retained orders and acquisition cost. A new Swedish supplier saving SEK 40 per kit adds only SEK 800 at twenty sales; useful, but not a solution to every uncertainty.

## What was checked in Sweden

Fresh research used the connected Chrome session. This extends earlier work, which concentrated on AW and the authenticated Candledust/Hertwill baskets. Search snippets were treated as leads; conclusions below come from the live merchant pages. No new supplier was contacted.

Swedish business, Swedish stock/dispatch and Swedish manufacture are different claims. None of the following establishes an equivalent **Swedish-manufactured, scented, plant-based, ready-packed** replacement at a lower confirmed wholesale landed price.

### 1. Sandig — strongest Swedish scented reseller lead

Sources: [reseller page](https://www.sandig.se/pages/bli-aterforsaljare), [scented product](https://www.sandig.se/products/sandljus-med-doft), [homepage](https://www.sandig.se/).

- Actual reseller programme invites new stores to contact kundtjanst@sandig.se; no public wholesale tariff, minimum or approved account.
- Merchant explicitly says dispatch is from its warehouse in Timrå, Sweden. Retail delivery claim 1–3 days; free retail freight above SEK 499. These are published claims, not a twenty-unit business quotation.
- Product contents: **500 g plant-based sand and 30 wicks**.
- Live product selector showed Tallskog available with “few left”; Lavendel, Naturell/Doftfri and Morgonbris marked sold out. No numeric allocation or twenty-pack availability verified.
- Retail product displayed SEK 399 per regular pack and a buy-two/get-four promotion with a total of SEK 798 for four 500 g packs. That is SEK 199.50 per 500 g under the displayed promotion. No cart or order was made, and no assumption that promotional discounts apply to wholesale.
- A retail gift kit promotion was also displayed. Promotional value is not a cost-saving quotation for Noorly.
- Footer identifies **Mhem AB**, Plåtslagarvägen 19, 86136 Timrå. Manufacturing country was not established.

**Useful new logistics lead:** this is the same street address previously published by Mitt Logistik (which identifies Swedish Treats AB). Shared address alone does not prove shared ownership, a supplier arrangement or free transfer. Ask whether they can supply the sand and fulfil the whole Noorly kit from that site, and itemise any transfer/receiving fees. Do not describe a combined service as available or agreed.

Qualification priority: scented sealed packs comparable with the 650 g/1 kg concept, wholesale rate, actual stock/dispatch, scent/wick documents, manufacturer origin, and whether an appropriate bundle/fulfilment arrangement exists. Current public retail pricing does not beat the authenticated Candledust cost.

Good small-batch reseller lead, but current pack size, unscented format and unknown trade price make it a weaker match for the preferred scented kit than Sandig.

### 3. Kristinas Scrapbooking / KeraSnow — local seller and genuine bulk option, different product

Sources: [1 kg](https://kristinasscrapbooking.se/stopa-ljus-gjuta-ljus/ljusmassa/kerasnow-snabbljus-i-ljusbehallare-1-kg/), [20 kg](https://kristinasscrapbooking.se/stopa-ljus-gjuta-ljus/ljusmassa/kerasnow-snabbljus-i-ljusbehallare-20-kg/), [onsite results](https://kristinasscrapbooking.se/search/?lang=sv&q=KeraSnow), [delivery terms](https://kristinasscrapbooking.se/info/kop-och-leveransvillkor/).

Live public prices:
- 1 kg SEK 138, in stock.
- 3 kg SEK 408.
- 5 kg SEK 670.
- 10 kg SEK 1,240.
- 20 kg SEK 2,280, in stock: **SEK 114/kg**, or SEK 74.10 raw wax for a 650 g portion before the extras below.

The 1 and 20 kg pages explicitly describe pouring powder into a fire-safe container and inserting a wick without melting; this is genuinely candle sand, not merely candle-making flakes.

However:
- Specifications say **paraffin/mineral wax, not vegetable wax**, origin **England**.
- Wicks and glass are explicitly sold separately.
- Sold by weight and supplied in bags; packaged resale portions, own-brand rights, exact supplier labels and documents require qualification.
- A larger bag would require portioning, bags/labels, weighing, extra labour, wick purchasing and product-document work.
- The retail price display offers inclusive/exclusive VAT modes. The exact selected tax basis was not independently confirmed from the visible controls. Treat amounts as displayed prices until the invoice/cart tax basis is verified; do not assume every figure is a final cash wholesale price.
- Delivery terms show Swedish collection in Ängelholm, but also mention a second warehouse in Denmark. Do not assume KeraSnow is physically on the Swedish shelf. “In stock” is not allocation of the required batch.
- Published free delivery above SEK 599 excludes home delivery; business/direct delivery conditions must be quoted. No twenty-kg delivery basket or reservation was obtained.

Bulk cash matters: twenty kits at 650 g need 13 kg. Buying 20 kg for SEK 2,280 leaves 7 kg unused. Allocating all the first purchase cash to twenty kits is SEK 114 per kit before wicks, packing and labour, not SEK 74.10. The onsite 10 kg + 3 kg displayed prices total SEK 1,648 for the required 13 kg, before any shipping or extras, but availability/tax/combined basket were not quoted.

This is a credible cost lead for a **different unscented/paraffin offer**, not an approved substitute for the pre-scented plant-based product. Do not add fragrance oil or repeat the merchant's broad safety claims without the full intended assembly being qualified.

## Sourcing recommendation and target

Retain Candledust as the current quoted scented baseline while qualifying **Sandig first** and **K. Lundqvist second**. No replacement is selected by this research.

Current Candledust Rhubarb Sparkle 650 g allocated supplier cash cost is approximately SEK 142.13, including the quoted Sweden freight/tax and the original sample allocation. It includes 17 wicks. It is an actual earlier account basket; the Swedish leads are currently public pages.

A Swedish quote should beat that amount on a comparable **finished sealed pack, sufficient sand, appropriate wicks, documents, stock and inbound delivery**, including nonrecoverable VAT. A proposed negotiating target of around **SEK 100–110 per comparable 650 g pack delivered** would produce a useful SEK 32–42 saving before small FX differences. This is Noorly's target, not an available supplier price.

Do not save SEK 40 on sand and lose the benefit through repacking, extra warehouse receiving, a higher parcel band or reduced perceived value. Do not assume supplying from Sweden eliminates VAT or domestic customer delivery.
