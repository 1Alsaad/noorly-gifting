## Cash available to reinvest is different

The table is a contribution/operating-surplus planning model before tax, with the stated allowances and reserves. It is not an exact month-end bank balance. No salary/owner withdrawal is budgeted. Product testing, one-time setup, timing of supplier bills, payout delays, refunds, prepaid annual expenses and unsold inventory can change cash.

If twenty kits' components are bought but only ten kits sold, cash is tied up in the remaining components even though the table charges product cost only against sold kits. Do not read ten-order break-even as saying that receipts alone finance a twenty-kit inventory buy. Reprice an actual ten-unit batch or separately fund unsold inventory. The outsourced receiving/storage charges may also fail to scale linearly.

Keep the damage/returns and tax provisions available rather than reinvesting every bank balance. Once the business form, VAT invoices, actual quote, packaging and supplier batch are settled, replace the assumptions with a cash schedule as well as a profit calculation.
