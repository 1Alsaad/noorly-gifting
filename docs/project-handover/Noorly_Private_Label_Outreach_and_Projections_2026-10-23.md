## What period and result are being projected?

These are **the first full selling month after a qualified, authorized launch**, not the remaining days of October or a prediction that the currently protected/unlaunched offer is already selling.

Price is **SEK 1,099 delivered per kit**. Orders mean completed, retained sales after cancellations/refunds, not page visits, deposits or raw checkout events. Presale cash is not treated as earned profit before fulfilment. No salary/owner draw is included.

Every scenario deducts:
- Product components and supplier delivery, including sand/wicks/pouch/paid filling where relevant.
- Outsourced order handling/packing, Swedish customer delivery, and a damage/returns allowance.
- Payment fees assumed at 3% of the customer payment + SEK 3 = **SEK 35.97/order**.
- **SEK 3,000 total monthly advertising**, deducted once.
- **SEK 2,000 total recurring overhead**, including the owner's ChatGPT/miscellaneous budget, deducted once.
- A new **SEK 1,000 first-month setup/testing allowance** shown explicitly. This is a modelling buffer, not a supplier quote, approved spend, proof that adequate tests cost only this amount, or a cap on required work.

Income/corporation tax is not calculated because the business form and registration are unsettled. Actual excess setup/testing, legal-label work, insurance, minimum warehouse fees or omitted recurring costs reduce profit further. If costs are deferred, they do not become free.

## VAT treatment: two explicit models

The owner is not yet business- or VAT-registered. Neither status alone determines exemption eligibility. A business intended to grow should also be assessed under an ordinary taxable-sales model.

1. **Eligible VAT-exempt case:** all SEK 1,099 is sales revenue; purchase VAT remains a cost. This is conditional, not an assertion that Noorly qualifies.
2. **VAT-registered planning case:** customer price stays SEK 1,099, but net sales revenue is **SEK 879.20** and output VAT is SEK 219.80/order. Eligible purchase VAT is removed from economic costs, assuming correctly addressed/issued business invoices and full business use. This is a prospective model, not a claim that the current foreign-VAT cart can be deducted on a Swedish return.

Specific registered-model arithmetic:
- Middle-case Candledust landed raw sand/wick/sample component SEK 135.30595596 is divided by 1.24 to infer the underlying net commercial cost from the actual 24%-VAT-inclusive basket, with the existing FX cushion retained proportionally. This assumes a future valid B2B transaction/invoice at unchanged net prices; it does NOT claim a Swedish deduction for Estonian VAT already charged.
- Middle-case local pouch/filling/labels SEK 31.95 and other eligible bowl/insert/fulfilment/delivery amounts are divided by 1.25. Returns/damage reserve stays SEK 15; no VAT credit is assigned to a reserve.
- Pessimistic and optimistic hypothetical quote amounts assume a 25% gross-to-net equivalent for taxable product/service lines, with their reserve unchanged. Actual supplier invoice rates/terms can change the outcome.
- Payment fees are left at SEK 35.97; no speculative input-tax recovery is applied.
- The mixed SEK 2,000 overhead, SEK 3,000 ad budget and SEK 1,000 startup allowance remain full economic cost allowances in both cases. We do not claim tax recovery on these unreconciled amounts. Correct invoices may improve the registered model, or omitted tax/charges may worsen it.
- Thus registered variable costs before payment fees are **SEK 492.00 / 450.04 / 372.17** respectively.

This is more useful than the previous output-VAT/no-input-recovery stress column, which was deliberately pessimistic and was never a normal registered-business profit calculation. Do not subtract all output VAT while simultaneously ignoring all eligible purchase-tax recovery and call that the expected result.

Official sources rechecked in connected Chrome:
- [Skatteverket: small-business VAT exemption](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/momsregistrering/ivissafallbehoverduinteregistreradittforetagformoms.4.3152d9ac158968eb8fd1efe.html): qualifying conditions, generally no input-tax deduction when exempt, and when exemption ends.
- [Skatteverket: buying goods/services](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/kopavarorochtjanster.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005374.html): input-tax deduction and special cross-border rules.
- [Skatteverket: EU goods purchases](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/kopavarorochtjanster/inkopfranandraeulander/kopavarorfranandraeulander.4.3a7aab801183dd6bfd380005738.html): valid VAT number and conditions for seller not charging foreign VAT, Swedish reporting and usual deduction for taxable business use. Registration solely to report certain purchases does not confer general deduction rights.

Do not annualise an exempt scenario without checking the applicable annual turnover conditions. The webpage also explains its tax-base calculation; the annual threshold must not be treated as a simple permanent exemption for any future sales volume.

## Which is most likely?

**Planning judgment: closer to the middle case than the optimistic case, with low confidence. Budget for roughly break-even or a modest first-month loss; do not budget to withdraw/reinvest a promised SEK 5,000–10,000 profit.**

A provisional working range of **10–15 retained sales** is reasonable for budgeting, not a statistically estimated confidence interval or a claim based on observed performance. At the middle unit costs and after the SEK 1,000 startup allowance:
- Eligible exempt outcome: approximately **−947 to +1,580 SEK**.
- Registered-model outcome: approximately **−2,068 to −102 SEK**.

Twelve orders is a centre-of-range planning input, not a forecast with an assigned probability. A five-order or zero-order outcome remains possible. No numerical probabilities are fabricated.

Why this cautious view:
- The product/creative/checkout have no validated conversion record for this exact high-price kit; a new brand must earn customer trust.
- A presale asks the buyer to wait, which can add friction. A firm dispatch date, actual sample photography and a clear demonstration of the sculptural bowl/insert may help, but that improvement is not measured.
- Small-batch own-label packing is currently costed above the old finished branded pack. Below SEK 100 is still a negotiation target.
- Fragile/heavy components, three supplier flows and warehouse minimums can raise costs. The current parcel weight, safe configuration and complete service quote are not final.
- Seasonal gifting/winter presentation could help the offer, but it is a qualitative hypothesis; no extra sales are booked for it.
- A successful twenty-order month requires SEK 150 ad spend per retained buyer on a SEK 3,000 budget. This is materially better than the middle case's SEK 250 and has not been demonstrated.

## What matters most and what would change the forecast

At the middle costs:
- Each extra retained sale adds about **SEK 505 exempt / SEK 393 registered** before any extra advertising or overhead.
- Reducing the finished sand from SEK 167.26 to 100 at twelve orders adds about **SEK 807** in the exempt model. Helpful, but not enough to make poor demand healthy.
- A SEK 30 unforeseen cost per order reduces twelve-order profit by SEK 360 before any relevant VAT treatment.
- A one-off charge exceeding the allowance by SEK 1,000 reduces profit by another SEK 1,000.
- Achieving twenty sales at unchanged middle costs gives approximately **SEK 4,106 exempt / SEK 1,864 registered** after startup. The optimistic cost reductions then improve those figures further.

Middle-cost operating break-even:
- Excluding first-month setup: **10 orders exempt / 13 registered**.
- Including the SEK 1,000 allowance: **12 orders exempt / 16 registered**.
- On the fixed SEK 3,000 ad budget, those first-month thresholds correspond to about SEK 250 / SEK 188 advertising cost per retained buyer. These are economic thresholds, not evidence we can acquire buyers at those costs.

Replace assumptions only when there is evidence: a complete finished-pack quote, written all-in fulfilment/parcel terms, qualified sample, valid invoices/tax setup, and observed paid-to-retained conversion. Presale interest without completed, fulfilled retained sales is weaker evidence.

An expanded thirty-sale upside at the same optimistic costs and budget would show SEK 32,970 customer payments and about **SEK 12,047 exempt / SEK 8,132 registered** after startup. It requires SEK 100 ad spend per retained order, more supply and a separately authorized batch beyond twenty. It is an upside sensitivity, not the main expectation or an expanded launch authorization.

## Cash available to reinvest

Profit is not the month-end bank balance. Buying twenty kits' stock and selling twelve leaves eight kits' components unsold.

At the middle gross cost basis, bowl + insert + finished sand are approximately **SEK 392.84 per kit**. Eight unsold equivalents tie up approximately **SEK 3,143**. Subtracting that from the exempt first-month operating profit of SEK 64 gives an illustrative **SEK 3,079 cash shortfall**, while the stock remains an asset. This bridge assumes all twenty sets are purchased at the same allocated costs; actual order minima, payout timing, fixed storage/receiving, VAT timing and unusable stock can change it. No analogous precise registered cash balance is claimed.

Presales may reduce how much inventory must be financed personally, but:
- customer payments are an obligation to deliver/refund, not free profit;
- payment-provider payout timing/holds, tax/refund reserves and supplier lead times still matter;
- a minimum batch and stock availability must be confirmed before promising delivery;
- orders/presales/ads remain closed pending the owner's separate release authorization.
