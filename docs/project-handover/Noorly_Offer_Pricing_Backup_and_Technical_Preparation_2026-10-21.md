## Price and allowable acquisition cost

All values SEK/order, rounded. Assumptions: retail price includes delivery and 25% VAT; 230.80225 goods/shared delivery from provisional Hertwill model; 31 payment/refund allowance. Excludes overhead, FX spread and unconfirmed charges. All acquisition costs are hypothetical.

- 399 price: 319.20 excluding VAT; 57.40 before ads; minus 62.60 after CAC 120. Even zero CAC does not leave a 20% contribution margin on revenue excluding VAT.
- 449 price: 359.20 excluding VAT; 97.40 before ads; minus 22.60 after CAC 120. Maximum CAC for 20% contribution before overhead: 25.56.
- 499 price: 399.20 excluding VAT; 137.40 before ads; 17.40 after CAC 120. Maximum CAC for 20% contribution before overhead: 57.56.
- 549 price: 439.20 excluding VAT; 177.40 before ads; 57.40 after CAC 120. Maximum CAC for 20% contribution before overhead: 89.56.
- 599 price: 479.20 excluding VAT; 217.40 before ads; 97.40 after CAC 120. Maximum CAC for 20% contribution before overhead: 121.56.

Formula: allowable CAC = price / 1.25 × (1 - target contribution margin) - goods/delivery - allowance. Contribution is not net profit.

A lower supplier cost alone does not remove the same-product retail price gap. Conversely, matching a low retail price can destroy paid-acquisition economics. A vessel, packaging, card, local service or other distinction must actually exist, be valued and be costed; none is silently included. No new bundle, price or supplier SKU was saved to Shopify.

## Swedish seasonal context and backup ranking

[Svensk Handel's 11 December 2025 release](https://via.tt.se/pressmeddelande/4185253/julklappar-for-245-miljarder-kronor-under-granen?lang=sv&publisherId=1718444) forecast 24.5 billion SEK Christmas-gift spending for 2025 and identified substantial price competition. This is a prior-year national context, NOT a 2026 forecast or a wheat-cushion-specific statistic. No 2026 Christmas category uplift has been assumed.

Research ranking, based on fit rather than a fabricated numerical confidence score:

1. Adult unscented microwave wheat cushion: strongest combination here of repeated winter search interest, visible Amazon purchase-ranking evidence and a distinct home-comfort use case. Candidate format: one cushion with verified removable washable cover, no extra gifts implied. Risks: low competing prices, shipping weight, heating/fire/burn instructions and documented product suitability. No pain-relief, stress-reduction or sleep claim.
2. Wool socks: strong winter timing, light/unbreakable and giftable, but size variants/returns and cheap multipack competition complicate a profitable standalone paid offer. Keep as second candidate, not a proposed candle-sand bundle.
3. Sleep mask: established interest but weaker November fit in this comparison, low ticket and substantial competition. Do not prioritise it for the reserve now.

Illustrative sourcing ceiling for the wheat-cushion candidate: at 399 SEK delivered, 25% VAT, 31 allowance, hypothetical CAC 100 and 20% contribution on revenue excluding VAT, goods/packing/fulfilment/delivery must total at most 124.36 SEK excluding recoverable VAT. At 449, ceiling 156.36. These are cost ceilings, not supplier quotes or recommended retail prices. Amazon's lower prices make 399–449 acceptance unproven. No qualified supplier, sample or final SKU exists yet; do not publish the concept as available.
