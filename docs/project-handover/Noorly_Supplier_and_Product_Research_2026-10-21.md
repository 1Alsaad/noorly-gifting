## Recommendation

Keep candle sand as a conditional first offer, while qualifying **one adult creative activity kit** and **one original Swedish guided journal** as alternatives. The kit has the strongest recent category-level trend evidence found; the journal has a practical route to own-brand production without buying a large batch. Neither has proven Swedish purchase demand or a verified profitable supplier quote.

For candle sand, compare AW with The Candledust's wholesale/bulk route and its suggested dropshipping intermediary, Hertwill. For finished candles, NAVA and MA CONCEPTION are more accessible private-label leads than a large custom-manufacturing programme. A conventional scented candle remains an adjacent home-fragrance proposition; changing scent, pack size, supplier or label must not be counted as a fresh independent product test.

**No researched route is fully cleared for a paid Friday launch.** We have public supplier evidence, not reserved stock, accepted Swedish landed quotes or approved samples. Friday can be a supplier/offer decision and technical setup checkpoint. A presale does not remove the need for credible fulfillment and complete checkout testing.

Exactly **4,000 SEK remains the total advertising allocation**, with up to 2,000 for the first screen and 2,000 reserved. Inventory, samples, packing materials, subscriptions and merchant overhead are separate costs; this research authorizes none of that spending. No ads were activated.

### Season timing

- Late October: supplier qualification, samples, cost comparison and technical preparation.
- November: a credible window for shorter private-label production, subject to artwork, deposit, sample and freight timing. Hosting and gift discovery are reasonable creative angles.
- Late November–early December: focus on products with confirmed stock and documented holiday capacity.
- December: advertise arrival before Christmas only if supported by the actual supplier/carrier cutoff and an operational buffer. Stop that promise when the cutoff passes.
- After the delivery cutoff: evergreen journals and home activities can still be sold with honest later dispatch; Christmas-specific decorations lose their purpose quickly.

Custom Advent calendars and a newly manufactured Halloween collection are poor fits for this timetable. Commodity electronics, ingestibles and cosmetics were not prioritised: they add qualification work without a verified margin or demand advantage here.

**Routes:** branded wholesale, bulk for repacking, and finished white-label pouches. The wholesale page says trade pricing starts at €50 order value, subject to an approved business account [S1]. That threshold is not a finished white-label minimum.

The programme page lists €1,500 + VAT goods value, 100 units per variation and 1,000 printed pouches per artwork; it describes two weeks once everything is at the factory [S2]. The FAQ permits bulk repacking, gives an indicative 48-hour dispatch plus 2–10 working days EU delivery for bulk, and says new white-label customers cannot use its direct dropshipping service; it points to Hertwill [S3]. **Verdict:** first direct alternative to quote. Prefer asking about a small batch of existing finished packs or a modest bulk pilot before committing roughly 16,685 SEK in goods to the stated €1,500 route. ## Cost ceilings: what the supplier numbers must allow

Working assumptions: 25% output VAT, recoverable input VAT where applicable, actual net costs, and **100 SEK contribution left after advertising per order** as an illustrative target. This is money available for overhead and owner time—not net profit or take-home pay. VAT registration, invoice treatment and fees remain to be verified. General Swedish VAT guidance is at [L1].

**Maximum all-in product/fulfillment cost = customer total ÷ 1.25 − payment/refund allowance − assumed CAC − target contribution.**

“All-in” includes product, inbound freight allocation, retail packaging/inserts, packing/handling labour, customer delivery, duty/clearance and unrecoverable taxes. Payment fees and expected refund losses are already separate allowances; avoid double counting.

| Offer scenario | Product + shipping SEK | Fees + refund allowance | Assumed CAC | Maximum all-in product/fulfillment cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Candle-sand pair | 499 + 99 = 598 | 31 | 100 | **247.40** |
| Five-pouch collection | 999 + 99 = 1,098 | 55 | 150 | **573.40** |
| Original guided journal | 499 + 49 = 548 | 30 | 100 | **208.40** |
| Creative kit for two | 599 + 59 = 658 | 35 | 125 | **266.40** |
| Personalised four-napkin set | 599 + 59 = 658 | 35 | 125 | **266.40** |
| Finished premium candle | 399 + 69 = 468 | 30 | 100 | **144.40** |
| Personalised blanket | 899 + 69 = 968 | 50 | 150 | **474.40** |

These are procurement ceilings, not supplier prices or CAC forecasts. Actual consumer acceptance comes first: a price that balances the model but does not convert is not viable.

Example: at 658 SEK collected, a creative kit with 250 SEK all-in product/fulfillment and 35 SEK fees/reserve leaves 241.40 SEK before ads. At CAC 75/125/200, the remainder is 166.40/116.40/41.40 SEK. If all-in cost becomes 320 SEK, those figures fall by 70 SEK. That is why delivered quotes matter.

The existing AW pair model is about 245.82 SEK for goods plus public shipping alone. Against a 247.40 SEK ceiling, it leaves only **1.58 SEK** for any additional packing/handling under the same assumptions. The proposed 499 + 99 price therefore has little extra-cost room if the target is 100 SEK after a 100 SEK CAC. At the original 449 + 99 proposal, the ceiling would be 207.40 SEK.

A five-pouch basket can show better contribution but requires a much larger purchase and may convert less often. Do not assume its CAC equals the pair's. Packed weight can push AW's five-pouch parcel above 2 kg; the existing report models that higher shipping band.

## White label: the responsibilities must be included in the quote

There are distinct commercial models:

1. **Plain parcel:** supplier branding is absent from the shipping carton, but the product can retain another brand.
2. **Finished own-brand goods:** the supplier makes and labels the product for Noorly, with agreed documentation.
3. **Noorly repacking:** we buy bulk, weigh/pack/label and arrange fulfillment ourselves.
4. **Original print on demand:** we provide the design/content; a provider manufactures each order.

EU guidance treats a business that has products made and markets them under its own name as a manufacturer [L2]. Consequently, a white-label quotation needs a clear responsibility/documentation agreement. Swedish candle resale guidance also requires appropriate Swedish labelling and highlights CLP duties [L3]. A supplier's broad “EU compliant,” “non-toxic” or “clean burning” marketing is not sufficient evidence for Noorly claims.

For shortlisted candles, request the exact formulation/variant documentation, applicable hazard classification and label text, batch traceability, safe-use information and test evidence. For kits, establish intended users and applicable material/safety instructions. For textiles, obtain composition/care information. Confirm which information must appear on the product and online listing. Do not add CE markings indiscriminately.
