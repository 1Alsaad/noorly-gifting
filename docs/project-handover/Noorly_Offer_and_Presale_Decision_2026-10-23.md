## Decision

**Qualify one distinctive wooden starter kit, then run a capped presale only after separate release authorization. Buy a consolidated batch into Sweden and send one finished parcel per customer. Sell refills primarily as add-ons and repeat purchases.**

The strongest current value concept is AWGifts TeakB-31 plus a suitable candle insert and one sealed Candledust Midnight 1 kg pack with 17 wicks. **SEK 1,149 delivered is the price to validate**, not an approved price, proven demand or a published offer. A 650 g configuration around SEK 1,099 is the lower-cost alternative if the physical sample establishes adequate fill and a better appearance. Choose one configuration after testing; do not split the small media budget among many scents, bowl sizes and offers.

The wood is the differentiating decorative object. A simple 3–4× markup on sand does not pay for a separate parcel, acquisition, handling and all taxes automatically. Presales reduce inventory exposure but do not repair weak contribution or guarantee immediate supplier funding.

**No wooden assembly is yet qualified for sale.** The large open sand surface in the reference cannot be assumed to work with a small insert or one pouch. If the tested insert ruins the intended sculptural appearance, or cannot be used safely, reject that assembly and continue sourcing. Do not conceal the actual insert in marketing or quietly substitute a generic glass product.

## VAT: corrected model after owner's answer

Not being VAT-registered is not, by itself, proof of eligibility for VAT-exempt sales. [Skatteverket's small-business guidance](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/momsregistrering/ivissafallbehoverduinteregistreradittforetagformoms.4.3152d9ac158968eb8fd1efe.html), read in Chrome, requires Swedish establishment and annual turnover within SEK 120,000 in the current and two previous calendar years. Its calculation section explains that the limit is the taxable base; for wholly 25%-rated supplies it gives SEK 150,000 in sales without charging VAT. Check the whole business, not Noorly orders alone.

If eligible and exempt, sales do not carry output VAT and input VAT is generally not deductible. This improves pilot contribution relative to the earlier model, but it is not a reason to price a scalable business as if VAT never applies.

[Skatteverket's registration guidance](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/momsregistrering/registreradittforetagformoms.4.deeebd105a602bfe38000256.html) also identifies foreign purchases that can require registration: relevant services taxed in Sweden and, in the stated circumstances, EU goods exceeding SEK 90,000/year. Resolve foreign ad/service billing before activation. Do not assume that small-business sales exemption makes foreign advertising free of VAT.

Below:
1. **Qualifying exempt pilot** means no output VAT, supplier cash costs retained.
2. **25% output-VAT stress case** subtracts output VAT but deliberately credits no input recovery. This is a conservative planning floor, **not Noorly's current tax treatment and not a final registered-business profit calculation**.
3. A proper registered-business model must use correct supplier invoices and allowable input VAT. It may improve on the stress case, but foreign VAT cannot simply be claimed as Swedish input VAT.

No registration, tax setting or invoice was changed.

## Presale and cash plan to prepare

1. **Qualify the prototype first.** Purchase needs separate authorization. Verify representative bowl variation, insert appearance/fit, exact wick/sand use, product documents, Swedish information, packed dimensions/weight and usable carrier quote. Do not sell an unqualified assembly to finance these checks.
2. Use the actual prototype to validate interest organically. Preserve the existing winter website film; any new product photos should show the exact included bowl, visible insert and sealed pack truthfully. No generated reference image should imply a larger sand surface than customers receive.
3. After payment/refund, stock/dispatch and release gates pass, propose one 4–5-day, **maximum 20-order** presale at the selected delivered price, with a clearly supported dispatch window and refund procedure. No launch or supplier stock hold is authorized now.
4. The current costing assumes 20 bought kits. If fewer customers commit, **requote the actual smaller batch** or refund under the agreed terms. Do not buy 20 units against ten orders, extend promises, or spend on residual inventory automatically.
5. Consolidate each supplier's batch to Noorly, inspect bowls and glass, pack each complete kit, then use one tracked domestic shipment per customer. Do not split wood and sand into separate international customer deliveries.
6. Treat 20 paid, non-cancelled orders as a target, not a forecast. SEK 2,000 media / 20 orders is SEK 100 media CAC; ten orders is SEK 200. If applicable non-recoverable ad VAT is 25%, SEK 100 platform spend becomes SEK 125 all-in CAC and reduces the table's contribution by SEK 25/order.
7. Review first results by day four and at roughly SEK 500 spend. Pause and investigate if there are no retained paid orders; do not spend the full test automatically to meet a target. This is a cash-control rule, not statistical proof of failure. Compare contribution using actual costs and all-in CAC.
8. Keep SEK 2,000 reserve untouched until a separate decision supported by conversion, actual delivery quality, returns and contribution. Total **media** allocation remains SEK 4,000 (2,000 initial / 2,000 reserve), with historical actual spend still unreconciled. No extra tax, stock or ad spending has been authorized.
