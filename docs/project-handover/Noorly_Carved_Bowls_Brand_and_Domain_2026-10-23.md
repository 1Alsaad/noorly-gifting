## Assessment of Noorly
Recommendation: keep Noorly as the working Swedish home-and-gifting brand and secure noorly.se if still available at the owner's checkout. The six-letter name is compact on a label, has a soft sound appropriate to the proposed aesthetic, and can accommodate bowls, candles and gifts. Any association with light/Nordic warmth is an interpretation, not a claimed Swedish dictionary meaning.

Limitations: pronunciation/spelling may need reinforcing because Noorly can be heard as Norly. It is not globally unique. Noorly.in sells Indian laundry products including scent-booster beads, and separate Noorly app/software businesses exist. Noorly.com is not available through the checked domain tools. This weakens global search exclusivity, without establishing an existing Swedish/EU same-goods trademark conflict.

This is a preliminary commercial assessment, not completed trademark clearance. Exact/similar marks in the relevant goods/services and Swedish territory still need PRV/EUIPO/WIPO checks before substantial irreversible branding commitments. Domain availability does not establish trademark rights. Do not report the name as legally cleared.
