# Noorly supplier shortlist and quote brief — 21 October 2026

This is a focused follow-up to the [supplier/product research](Noorly_Supplier_and_Product_Research_2026-10-21.md) and [presale handover](Noorly_Presale_State_v1.2.md). It prioritises suppliers for a small Swedish test and a conditional November expansion. A shortlist is not an approved purchase, verified sample or launch clearance.

## Decision

**First qualify The Candledust's existing scented products/bulk route and Hertwill's resale route. In parallel, qualify Devillebard for finished own-brand candle sand.** LEDOM is a useful lower-cost unscented-kit lead, but does not currently establish an equivalent pre-scented pouch. Creativ Company is the strongest operational lead for the separate adult activity-kit fallback. Prodigi remains the clearest researched route for an original Noorly journal.

The public research has not established a supplier combining all four: low pilot quantity, finished Noorly branding, confirmed November stock, and an accepted Swedish delivered cost below our target. No candidate is cleared for paid orders.

The 4,000 SEK total advertising allocation is unchanged: up to 2,000 for the initial screen and 2,000 reserved. Samples, inventory, subscriptions and packaging are separate expenses, with no new spending authorised here. Friday 23 October remains a setup/decision checkpoint.

## What best fits this launch

Prefer existing formulations and a small selection. Obtain both a pilot and a repeat-order quote. Compare a batch delivered to Noorly in Sweden with individual customer fulfillment; include all additional work and delivery costs.

- A practical pilot is a quote for the supplier's smallest available batch, with 50 and 100 pouches as comparison quantities, not commitments.
- Ask whether approximately 340 pouches could be supplied across November if demand validates. This is conditional capacity, not a sales forecast or purchase order.
- Compare 300 g and standard 350 g formats separately. Do not force a custom size that raises minimums.
- Ask for one-, two- and five-pouch parcels, including mixed variants and packed weights. Quote the premium collection separately.
- Obtain the precise scent/colour, wick count and appropriate vessel specification. A separate fragrance-oil bottle is not equivalent to pre-scented candle sand.
- Finished compliant packaging is preferable for a fast pilot. Bulk repacking requires costed labour, suitable packs, traceability and appropriate Swedish product documentation.

## Shortlist

### 1. The Candledust, Estonia — closest product match; favour a small existing-product or bulk pilot

The [wholesale page](https://thecandledust.com/wholesale/) states a €50 opening threshold. The [B2B FAQ](https://thecandledust.com/b2b-white-label-faq/) permits own-brand repacking of bulk sold by kilogram, with published bulk dispatch within 48 hours and EU transit of 2–10 working days. These are supplier statements, not reserved stock or a Sweden delivery commitment.

The [finished white-label programme](https://thecandledust.com/the-candledust-white-label-program-candle-sand/) lists €1,500 + VAT in goods, 100 units per variation and 1,000 printed pouches per artwork. Its own-packaging route and two-week factory stage differ from the FAQ's 1,000-piece/3–4-week description. Obtain one written specification and schedule; do not choose the cheapest interpretation.

**Fit:** scented sand, EU production and an explicitly permitted bulk branding route. **Gap:** exact trade prices, sample, available quantities, packing work and Sweden freight. Large finished white-label runs are poorly matched to unproven demand. New white-label dropshipping is unavailable according to the FAQ.

Contact: **hello@thecandledust.com**, confirmed on the [contact page](https://thecandledust.com/contact-the-candledust-pearled-candle/).

Ask for: existing 350 g packs versus 5–15 kg bulk pilots, two verified scents, complete documentation, standard unprinted packs/filling options, and a realistic November replenishment schedule.

### 2. Hertwill, Estonia — best researched candle-sand fulfillment route to qualify

Hertwill offers Shopify fulfillment and [Sweden shipping](https://hertwill.com/resources/shipping). Its overview lists €7.99–€13.99 excluding VAT for standard Swedish parcels and general EU transit of 3–8 business days. Those ranges are not a Candledust-specific quote. Same-brand bundle shipping and presale holds require confirmation.

[Pricing](https://hertwill.com/pricing): free tier permits five products and ten monthly orders; Standard is €24.99/month on monthly billing. A larger November volume would exceed the free order allowance.

New search listings for [Nordic Lights 350 g](https://hertwill.com/product/nordic-lights-scented-candle-sand-refill-350g) and [Midnight 350 g](https://hertwill.com/product/midnight-scented-candle-sand-refill-350g) say in stock and require login for prices. Direct page opens failed during this check, so neither authenticated stock nor a purchasable Sweden quote was verified. Do not extrapolate the earlier out-of-stock 330 g Rhubarb SKU to every product.

**Fit:** existing scented products and potential order automation. **Gap:** goods retain their manufacturer brand; Noorly product white label is not established, and freight can erase the margin.

Contact: **hello@hertwill.com**.

Ask for: the two exact 350 g SKUs, two/five mixed packs in one parcel, logged-in prices, package branding, stock reservation and safe manual order release.

### 3. Devillebard, France — strongest new finished own-brand candle-sand lead, subject to quote

The supplier's [brand-creator page](https://devillebard.com/en/solutions/bulk-fragrance) explicitly offers bulk wax beads and finished white-label wax-bead candles, with a MOQ grid supplied during sampling. Numeric minimums and prices are not published.

The [main site](https://devillebard.com/en) advertises French production and dispatch within 24 hours across Europe. This does not establish the production time for a custom Noorly order or Swedish arrival. Its [legal notice](https://devillebard.com/en/legal) identifies DEVILLEBARD SAS, SIREN 993 104 181; this is a website identity disclosure, not an independent company/quality audit.

**Fit:** a potential ready-packed Noorly product, with supplier documentation support. **Gap:** finished pack sizes, actual pre-scented sand options, minimums, costs, production capacity and direct-to-consumer fulfillment all need confirmation. The page says its ambient fragrances are for diffusion, not mixing into wax; do not treat those oils as a validated candle-scent system.

Contact: **info@devillebard.com**.

Ask for: minimum finished own-brand pouch/kit quantity, current candle-sand formulations, sample cost, complete Swedish-market documentation, and separate standard-stock versus custom-production dates.

### 4. LEDOM, Poland — promising unscented kit/bulk option; a product-format adjustment

The [wholesale page](https://e-ledom.pl/en/wholesale/) supports small and large B2B orders and custom candle-kit packaging, without a numeric MOQ.

The [catalogue](https://e-ledom.pl/en/catalog/) lists pearl wax at 35.49 PLN for 1 kg and 58.99 PLN for 2 kg including VAT, before unverified Swedish freight. These are public catalogue prices, not our trade or landed quote. Its 1 kg kits contain a separate oil bottle. The [FAQ](https://e-ledom.pl/en/faq/) confirms the wax itself is fragrance-free.

**Fit:** possible low-cost base for an unscented starter kit. **Gap:** pre-scented equivalence, Sweden shipping, custom-pack minimums, documentation and actual stock. Do not use the 25 kg general stearin listing as a validated substitute for the ready-to-use pearl product.

The [shipping policy](https://e-ledom.pl/en/shipping-policy/) mixes German delivery wording with PLN prices; its FAQ contains placeholder text. Obtain written clarification and a sample before relying on it. Polish and German site footers show different addresses under the same company identifiers.

Contact: **ledomstar@gmail.com**, published on its sites.

### 5. Candlera, Lithuania — unscented resale/kit backup

[Wholesale](https://candleracandle.eu/pages/wholesale) explicitly accepts retail resellers. Its [shipping policy](https://candleracandle.eu/pages/return-policy) says orders ship from Lithuania through FedEx, with a general Europe estimate of 2–5 business days.

The [FAQ](https://candleracandle.eu/pages/f-a-q) describes the sand as unscented. Neither white-label permission nor wholesale MOQ/prices was established. Potentially useful for an unscented styling kit; it does not currently replace the scented-pouch offer.

Contact: **hello@candleracandle.com**, confirmed on its [contact page](https://candleracandle.eu/pages/contact).

### 6. NAVA — transparent small-batch private-label finished candles

The [private-label page](https://navacandles.de/private-label) lists a minimum of 50 per vessel and scent. A 100 g tin is €4.50 at 50 units, including a standard label; 180 ml coloured glass is €8.50. Boxes and onward shipping are extra; invoice VAT basis requires clarification. Terms describe 50% deposit, roughly two weeks production after approval/payment and about another week for delivery.

**Fit:** comparatively accessible own-brand finished candle, with public price tiers. **Gap:** it is a different candle format, requires stock capital and may reach only part of November. Production origin and firm schedule need confirmation; a German warehouse does not establish German manufacture.

Contact: **jane@navacandles.de**. Five scents at the minimum would require 250 candles; one scent is a smaller pilot. A scent/supplier change does not count as our separate product test.

### 7. The Friendly Home, Sweden — domestic private-label quotation lead

Its [private-label page](https://thefriendlyhome.se/pages/private-label) offers scented candles and says production occurs on Värmdö outside Stockholm. It advertises low minimums but publishes no quantity, cost or production schedule.

**Fit:** local communication and an own-brand production route. **Gap:** no candle-sand offering was established and small-pilot economics remain unknown.

Contact: **private-label@thefriendlyhome.se**. Compare one existing formulation and simple packaging with NAVA before considering bespoke development.

“All-in” covers goods, inbound freight allocation, retail packaging, handling/packing labour, customer delivery, currency spread and unrecoverable tax/duties. Do not count payment/refund costs twice. Actual tax treatment, processor fees and return losses still need verification.

**Target 200–220 SEK per delivered pair.** A cost above 228.20 SEK fails this particular 100 SEK-contribution target at the assumed CAC; it is not a universal product-failure threshold.

At the old planning FX of 11.123 SEK/EUR, Hertwill's €7.99 shipping leaves approximately €5.89 per pouch inside a 220 SEK two-pouch cost ceiling. At €13.99 shipping, it leaves only €2.89 per pouch. These ceilings exclude any additional per-order charge. This explains why a real mixed-pair quote is essential; operational convenience is insufficient.

Ask about a five-pouch parcel separately. The earlier AW model's low shipping band is not established for its packed weight. A 1,099 SEK delivered five-pack, 55 SEK fee/refund allowance, 150 SEK assumed CAC and 200 SEK desired contribution allows **474.20 SEK** total goods/fulfillment cost. These are different assumptions from the pair, not measured five-pack economics.

## Quote brief ready for authorised outreach

Purpose: Swedish ecommerce pilot followed by conditional November replenishment. No committed sales volume; no orders accepted until supplier and checkout verification pass.

1. Specify existing scented and unscented 300/350 g finished products, included wicks and any ready-made suitable-vessel kit. Separate ready-stock products from new production.
2. Quote the smallest pilot, plus 50/100 pouches and a conditional 340-pouch November capacity. State minimums per scent, pack/artwork and reorder.
3. Quote delivery to one Swedish address and separate one-, two- and five-pouch customer parcels. Include mixed variants, packed dimensions/weight, handling, VAT, delivery method and surcharges.
4. State what can carry Noorly branding: label, pouch, outer box, card. Provide the branding/repacking rights and documentation for the actual specification.
5. Confirm sample terms, available stock, reservation mechanism, dispatch range, holiday capacity and reorder lead time. Distinguish production from transit.
6. Provide payment/deposit terms, cancellation before release, faulty-item replacement, return address and responsibility for refund/return costs.
7. Describe Shopify/manual/API routing, ability to hold presales, stock updates, idempotency/duplicate prevention and tracking feedback.
8. For candle products, provide product-specific safety documents, Swedish warning/label support and tested vessel/wick instructions. Supplier marketing claims about no fire risk, non-toxicity, health or universal container compatibility are not approved Noorly claims.

Each quotation needs a destination/postcode and the correct business/VAT details before it can be treated as final. Do not invent those fields. New outbound messages have not been sent.
