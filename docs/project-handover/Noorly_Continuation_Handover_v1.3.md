- Contribution before ads: **228.20**.
- At hypothetical media CAC 120: **108.20/order** left before fixed costs.
- At CAC 180: **48.20/order**.
- At CAC 230: **-1.80/order**.

CAC = actual media spend divided by consistently attributed verified new paying customers; 4,000 SEK is an allocation, not CAC. Increasing the budget does not guarantee lower CAC. Do not assume repeat purchases to justify losing money on the first order.

At 120 CAC and a desired 100 SEK/order before fixed costs, the maximum goods/fulfilment cost is **228.20**. The preferred 200–220 target gives some headroom, not a guaranteed viable business.

Risk/reward recommendation: a tightly bounded, low-preparation-cost test can be worthwhile after launch gates pass. Large white-label inventory and an aggressive 100k push are not justified by current evidence. No credible probability of success or full-project ROI percentage can yet be assigned.

Worst downside includes media plus samples, subscriptions, stock, packaging, returns/refunds and owner time. Track those separately; the 4,000 SEK media allocation is not the maximum possible loss. A zero-purchase first screen can lose its full 2,000 SEK media allowance plus preparation expenses.
