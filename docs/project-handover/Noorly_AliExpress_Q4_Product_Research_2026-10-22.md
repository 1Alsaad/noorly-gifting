# Noorly — AliExpress Q4 2026 product research

Research date: 22 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm. Market: Sweden. Status: research candidates; no selected supplier or launch approval.

## Decision

Qualify a complete adult craft kit first, with a quality adult blanket hoodie as the second sourcing candidate. Keep a pet-hair cleaning bundle as an evergreen alternative. Book nooks have relevant Swedish advertising activity but the researched listings need better sourcing economics. Deprioritise generic rechargeable hand warmers and unqualified candle-warmer lamps.

This is an analyst ranking for Noorly's budget, brand and fulfilment needs, not evidence that any exact AliExpress SKU is a profitable winner. The prior wheat-cushion fallback is not replaced or approved by this report.

The standing total media allocation is 4,000 SEK across the test sequence, initially up to 2,000 SEK for the first offer with a 2,000 SEK reserve. Historical spend must be reconciled. A new candidate does not create another budget. Ads and paid presales remain closed.

## Demand and competition

Etsy's Fall/Winter 2026 report, published 14 October with data as of 14 September, reports DIY activities searches up 8,500% and beginner sewing-pattern searches up 125%. The figures use U.S. signed-in Etsy activity and year-on-year comparisons over the preceding three months. They support a craft-night hypothesis, not Swedish sales volume or exact punch-needle demand. Its earlier 2026 report also reports beginner needlepoint kits up 175%; needlepoint and punch needle are different crafts.

Panduro lists its complete Learn & Create punch-needle kit at 499 SEK with substantial included materials. A cheap AliExpress needle/tool assortment must not be represented as equivalent. Noorly would need verified contents, clear Swedish instructions and an outcome beginners can actually achieve.

Hoodly lists a branded oversized comfort garment at 699 SEK and a hoodie dress at 599 SEK. Its material/design are not established as identical to the AliExpress fleece listing. Amazon.se search results also show a wearable blanket around 288.54 SEK. A 499 SEK hypothesis therefore requires a demonstrably good product and presentation; a competitor's premium price is not evidence customers will pay Noorly that price.

A Swedish INF roller comparison was 129 SEK across five retailers in the indexed PriceRunner result. Lufsen's own roller page displayed 349 SEK and a sold-out marker. Neither price proves sales. The low-price option means a generic single roller has a weak premium case.

Kjell's own category page displayed a hand-warmer/powerbank at 199.90 SEK, 224 reviews and availability in stores. This is competition evidence rather than an exact match to the researched two-pack. Its established retail service weakens a generic long-delivery import offer.

A GOV.UK September 2026 notice recalled a specific TikTok candle warmer for serious fire risk. A separate July report concerned electric-shock risk. These are model-specific notices; this report does not assert that all candle warmers or the AliExpress lead are unsafe.

## Cost limits for qualification

Illustrative model only: 25% output VAT, recoverable input VAT where actually applicable, 100 SEK media CAC, 30 SEK payment/refund allowance per order, and a desired contribution after media equal to 20% of revenue excluding VAT. These are not observed CAC, tax advice or final prices. Fixed costs and owner time remain outside this contribution.

Maximum delivered product/fulfilment cost = (delivered retail price / 1.25) x 0.80 - 100 - 30.

| Offer hypothesis | Customer total incl. delivery | Maximum delivered product/fulfilment cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| Single complete adult craft kit | 399 SEK | 125.36 SEK |
| Genuine two-person craft offer | 599 SEK | 253.36 SEK |
| Quality adult blanket hoodie | 499 SEK | 189.36 SEK |
| Useful pet-hair cleaning bundle | 349 SEK | 93.36 SEK |
| Book nook at a 599 SEK hypothesis | 599 SEK | 253.36 SEK |

At CAC 150, each cost ceiling falls by 50 SEK. At CAC 100, maximum break-even delivered costs are 189.20, 349.20, 269.20, 149.20 and 349.20 SEK respectively. Break-even is before fixed costs; it is not an acceptable target margin.

Delivered cost must include the exact goods/variants, FX/card spread, freight and any unrecoverable import charges, packing, handling, kitting, Swedish guide cost, customer delivery and non-recoverable taxes. Do not subtract VAT from an AliExpress consumer receipt without checking actual recoverability. AliExpress's retail tax collection does not establish Noorly's resale/import tax treatment. No currency conversion was used to turn a historical USD headline into a Swedish landed quote.

The USD 26–44 book-nook leads are concerning against a 253.36 SEK delivered-cost ceiling; the exact FX, tax and freight calculation is still required. A large apparent “buy for 100, sell for 499” spread is not contribution after VAT, ads and fulfilment.

## Concrete next qualification

1. Confirm the exact full adult variant and repeat-order price with Sweden selected, including any new-customer promotion restriction. Inspect recent photo reviews, negative reviews, seller history and order recency; do not rely on lifetime orders alone.
2. Obtain a postcode-specific tracked-delivery quote and stock position. Prefer a realistic roughly 7–10-business-day customer offer only if evidence supports it; no such ETA has been established. A sample delivery is needed before a dependable promise.
3. Confirm what is in each kit and that any two-person offer contains two usable sets. Verify patterns/imagery/resale rights, packed weight and damage/return handling.
4. For the hoodie, measure adult dimensions and assess fabric, seams, wash results and packed freight. For book nooks, check parts, assembly instructions, lighting and replacement-part support.
5. Inspect the sample and make original hands-only demonstrations before choosing the test. Sample purchase, supplier contact, inventory and paid activation need their separate standing authorisation.
6. Choose only one fallback offer within the existing reserve. The first candidate is the complete adult craft kit if landed cost can meet the ceiling and the sample/instructions pass. Otherwise qualify the hoodie next; no automatic spending follows from this ranking.

Christmas-only products lose much of their relevance after the delivery cutoff. Do not promise arrival before Christmas from an estimated marketplace banner, or plan Halloween sales this late in October on an untested China-to-Sweden route.
