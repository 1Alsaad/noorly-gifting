### Candle-use qualification

None of these wooden fruit/decor bowls has been qualified for direct burning of candle sand. Candledust's instructions specify a non-flammable container. The viable research direction is to preserve the sculptural wooden exterior with a suitable non-flammable insert, subject to testing the complete assembly.

The glass insert does not automatically make the wood-and-glass combination suitable. The real sample must establish fit, stability, clearance from the wood, rim geometry, heat transfer and performance through the intended burn cycle. Do not sell it as candle-safe merely because the insert is sold for candle making.

[Candle Shack clear 50cl Lotti, GLAS0501](https://candle-shack.com/products/50cl-bowl-clear) remains a component candidate:

- EUR 3.07 ex VAT for 1–59; EUR 2.77 for 60–119. Page displayed 314 available.
- Internal diameter 11.6 cm; outer top diameter 12.5 cm; outer height 7.03 cm; internal height 6.19 cm.
- Actual 20-unit cart: EUR 61.40 excluding VAT. Sweden checkout showed EUR 15.35 estimated tax and EUR 76.75 before freight. The product/cart's generic 20%-VAT display was superseded by the Swedish checkout's 25% estimate.
- Shipping required contact/full delivery information before the next step. No information was entered and no Sweden freight quote was obtained.
- Its roughly 425 g poured-wax capacity and three-wick poured-candle description do not establish loose-sand mass or a safe three-wick sand configuration.

The insert is taller than the published 5 cm leaf bowl and may be conspicuous or unstable in an irregular rough-bowl cavity. No promise of flush fit is justified.

## Sand quantity and packing: evidence versus assumptions

- 12 cm inside diameter × 5 cm filled depth: displayed 0.57 kg.
- 20 cm inside diameter × 5 cm filled depth: displayed 1.57 kg.

These are calculator outputs, not measurements of the AW bowl, the Lotti insert or the final kit. A broad wooden bowl can need much more sand than one pouch if the entire surface is filled. A smaller insert controls the burning area but changes the appearance. Neither 350 g, 650 g nor 700 g is yet a verified minimum fill for the final assembly.

At SEK 999 / CAC 100, contribution is about 14.4% of ex-VAT revenue. Under these assumptions, approximately SEK 1,073 delivered is required for 20% contribution at CAC 100; SEK 1,099 is a scenario, not a recommended validated market price. Demand at that price has not been demonstrated.

Sensitivity:

- Every SEK 10 extra freight, packaging, damage or acquisition cost reduces contribution by SEK 10.
- The >3–5 kg parcel band reduces contribution by SEK 20.
- The observed 650 g basket would save about SEK 6.80 per kit before small FX effects; it supplies 50 g less and uses a different scent/format. Fill sufficiency still needs checking.
- AW's one-time discount disappearing adds EUR 22.80 ex VAT across 20 bowls (EUR 28.50 including the displayed 25% VAT), about SEK 15.85 cash per kit before FX.
- Recoverable supplier VAT could improve the net-cost result, but no such recovery has been credited. The displayed AW/Candledust/insert tax together is roughly SEK 70 per kit; eligibility, invoice treatment and foreign VAT must be resolved before using that improvement.

These figures exclude fixed Shopify/app charges, product development/testing, creative production, general administration and income tax. The returns provision is not proof that those liabilities are fully funded. This is contribution, not net profit.
