# Noorly outsourced fulfilment assessment and quote requests

Date: 23 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm

## Recommendation

**Mitt Logistik is the first candidate to obtain a written quote from.** Its public calculator accepts our actual twenty-order pilot and gives a promising packing/storage price. Expresspack is the second candidate because it explicitly offers kit assembly and Swedish fulfilment. No provider has supplied a binding or product-specific Noorly quote; no supplier was contacted.

Prefer an outsourced twenty-kit batch if the full cost is at or below **SEK 159 per dispatched order including nonrecoverable VAT**, receiving, assembly, suitable protective packaging, storage, integration/setup allocation and domestic delivery, plus any increase in supplier inbound freight. At a similar economic cost, outsourcing fits the owner's preference and removes routine packing work. Do not select a provider solely on a basic pick fee.

This is a recommendation of whom to qualify, not proof that outsourcing is cheaper. The physical wood/insert/sand assembly still needs qualification before selling.

## Comparison with the manual model

The [previous cost assessment](Noorly_Cost_Reduction_Assessment_2026-10-23.md) assumed SEK 30 packing + SEK 20 owner handling + SEK 109 Swedish delivery = SEK 159 per order. The SEK 20 represents six minutes at SEK 200/hour; it is an economic cost, not necessarily cash paid to someone.

Thus manual cash outlay for these three lines is about SEK 139, versus economic cost SEK 159. A hypothetical outsourced SEK 149.88 is SEK 9.13 cheaper economically but SEK 10.88 more in immediate cash if the owner would otherwise work unpaid. At twenty orders that is roughly SEK 183 economic saving and SEK 218 additional cash expenditure. Neither model includes unmeasured extra trips/time.

The comparison retains the prior SEK 15 damage/returns reserve separately. Do not remove it merely because a warehouse offers returns handling or insurance. Avoid double counting charges if a subsequent quote includes a genuinely equivalent allowance.

Vi undersöker en liten pilot för Noorly och vill jämföra en komplett offert för lager och orderhantering. Detta är en prisförfrågan; vi har inte beslutat om inköp eller lansering.

Komponenterna kan komma direkt från tre EU-leverantörer. För en första kalkyl kan ni anta tre inleveranser med totalt ungefär sex kartonger och högst en pallplats under en månad, men de faktiska inleveranskollina behöver bekräftas.

Kan ni lämna ett specificerat totalpris för:

- Uppstart, eventuell Shopify-koppling eller alternativ manuell orderimport samt alla minimiavgifter, bindnings- och uppsägningsvillkor.

Ange priser både exklusive och inklusive moms, eventuella undantag för ömtåligt gods samt vilka uppgifter ni behöver för en fast offert. Noorly är ännu inte momsregistrerat. Produkt- och förpackningsprovning pågår; vi önskar en kommersiell bedömning av hanteringen, inte ett godkännande av produktens användning.
