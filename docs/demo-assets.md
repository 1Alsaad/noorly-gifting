# Preview asset provenance

The five WebP photographs in this repository are fictional static placeholders created for this project with the built-in image-generation tool. They are not photographs of actual supplier products, a real property, or a final brand campaign. No logo was generated. The winter scenes now use separately supplied Higgsfield videos; their provenance and encoding are recorded in [cinematic-media.md](cinematic-media.md). Ten catalogue SVG files are code-authored placeholder packaging illustrations.

| File                                                  | Purpose                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `public/media/winter/entrance-poster-desktop.webp`    | Landscape Nordic exterior and open doorway   |
| `public/media/winter/entrance-poster-mobile.webp`     | Separately composed portrait exterior        |
| `public/media/winter/candle-room-poster-desktop.webp` | Landscape cabin reading corner               |
| `public/media/winter/candle-room-poster-mobile.webp`  | Separately composed portrait reading corner  |
| `public/media/products/gift-box-editorial.webp`       | Fictional open gift box editorial still life |

The generated PNG sources were copied into project WebP assets using format conversion and proportional resizing, without semantic editing. Catalogue SVGs are intentionally distinguishable from real product photography. Replace every placeholder with approved merchant assets before opening real orders. Product specifications and contents must match the approved photographs.

## Prompt set

1. **Desktop entrance:** Cinematic landscape editorial photograph of a Scandinavian timber cabin entrance in Nordic winter blue hour. Already-open oak doorway reveals a softly lit interior, linen armchair and candle. Snowy stone steps, spruce branches, natural timber, desaturated teal exterior and honey interior light. Authentic architectural photography with dark negative space at left for HTML headlines. No people, text, logo, illustration or CGI.
2. **Mobile entrance:** Separate vertical 2:3 editorial composition of an open oak cabin door in winter blue hour. Snowy steps and fir branches, candlelit interior and linen armchair, doorway upper right and dark lower area for HTML text. No people, words or logos.
3. **Desktop interior:** Wide cinematic photograph of a Scandinavian cabin reading corner in winter. Snowy firs through a large left window, oak side table at right with one unbranded amber candle, linen armchair and wool blanket. Restrained warm light, muted forest green and brown, darker lower-left space for HTML type. No people, text or logos.
4. **Mobile interior:** Separate vertical 2:3 photograph of a cabin reading corner. Tall left window with snowy firs, central linen armchair, right oak table and lit amber candle with a blank ivory label. Warm honey light, wool textures and darker lower space for HTML type. No people, words, logos or CGI.
5. **Gift box:** Square editorial product still life of an open ivory gift box with burgundy ribbon, amber candle with blank label, unbranded tea pouch, kraft-wrapped chocolate and linen. Light stone surface, pine sprig, directional daylight and tactile paper textures. Full box visible with generous margins. No people, words or logos.

These prompts record the art direction; generation is not deterministic. Final video requirements and replacement paths are in [cinematic-media.md](cinematic-media.md).
