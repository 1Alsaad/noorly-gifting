# Noorly gift-box assortment research

Reviewed 19 October 2026 for a Swedish store selling in SEK. Recommendations below distinguish measured category momentum from product fit. A supplier listing, bestseller badge or social view count does not prove that a specific SKU will sell for Noorly. No supplier order, paid subscription or inventory commitment is made by this research.

## Recommended starting assortment

Start with a small assortment that supports three coherent gifts: a quiet evening, a writing ritual, and a tea ritual. Prefer compact products that feel useful together rather than adding unrelated viral items. Keep retail prices and availability unset until supplier terms and physical stock are known.

## Suggested box recipes

- **Quiet evening:** candle sand + notebook + personal card. Offer a separately verified candle vessel when available. Keep the pouch sealed and test the loaded parcel.
- **Writing ritual:** notebook + premium tea + personal card. This can offer a lower-fragility starting point than ceramics.
- **Tea ritual:** matcha or premium tea + the matching preparation accessory + card. Add the ceramic bowl only to a box with a tested protective insert.
- **Make a pause:** a complete adult craft kit + tea + card. Show the finished project, included materials and expected activity clearly.

These are proposed recipes, not tested shipping configurations. Measure actual internal box space, inserts and loaded weight. Scented products and food need separately sealed packaging and a pack test before combining them.

## Buying and measurement

Order samples and ask for unit costs, case quantities, minimum orders, lead times, image permission and replenishment terms. Calculate contribution from net sales less landed product cost, packaging, fulfilment, payment costs, shipping subsidy and expected damage/returns. Do not infer profit from supplier RRP.

Test the writing and quiet-evening recipes first. Measure product views → option selection → builder completion → paid checkout, then contribution and returns. Compare recipes within the same traffic source and device segment. Platform growth percentages are selection signals; only Noorly's own purchasing data can validate demand.
